# MAZ WORKS — LINKEDIN VIDEO PACK: "THE PRINTER SERIES"
Built 25 Sep 2026. Pairs with maz-works-linkedin-kit.md (posts) and maz-works-client-lead-brief.md (outbound).

## THE IDEA (one line)
Film yourself fixing a £200 3D printer the same way you fix small-business websites: **back up → measure → fix one thing → prove it.** The printer is the visual hook; the method is the pitch.

Why it works: people stop for machines moving; small-business owners trust someone who measures before charging. Every episode ends on the same bridge line:
> "That's exactly how I fix websites for small businesses. Measure it, fix it, prove it — fixed price, demo before you pay."

## RULES THAT DECIDE REACH (2026, researched)
- Shoot **vertical 9:16, 1080×1920**, MP4 H.264, 30 fps, under 200 MB.
- **Captions burned in** (≈85% watch on mute). 2–3 words per line, centre of frame. CapCut auto-captions, then proofread "EnderForge", "Marlin", "£299".
- **Hook in the first 3 seconds.** No "hi, I'm Maz". Open on motion + on-screen text.
- Post **natively from your personal profile**, then reshare from the Maz Works page.
- **No link in the post.** Put mazos-site link in the first comment.
- Caption under the video: 10–20 short lines (longer captions measured ~1.1× reach vs <5 lines ~0.7×). End with a question.
- Post **Tue–Thu, 9–11am**. Reply to every comment inside the first hour.
- Stay on one lane: "measure, fix, prove" for small businesses. The printer is always tied back to that.

## HONESTY RULE
Only say numbers that are in the EnderForge ledger. Placeholders like **[X]** get filled from `testing/ender-5/LEDGER.md` after the real test. No invented speed-ups.

- Get: 1 wide, 2 close-ups, 1 hand shot, 1 screen recording (Win+Alt+R or OBS).

- Export 1080×1920; captions burned in; first frame = motion + text.

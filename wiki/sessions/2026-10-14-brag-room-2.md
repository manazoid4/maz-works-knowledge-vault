# Brag Room 2.0: Maz Works video system

**Outcome.** Flagship (22 s, 4:5), 9 s hook and 9:16 vertical built with HyperFrames + /brag. New concept "Nobody's chasing it": enquiry answered, quote followed up, booking confirmed, invoice chased. Sells the whole chase, not one £149 task. No prices on screen; CTA "Get my free demo". Homepage swap is mazos-site PR #152 (awaiting Maz).

**Learnings**
- A personal prospect video must never assert a fact about the business it can't prove (v2 said "X just missed a call"). Evidence-first instead: one observed public fact with source and date, then "A proposal for X. Nothing is live."
- Public job adverts for admin roles are the strongest evidence pattern for automation leads, but they go stale fast: most saved adverts were gone or paused within weeks. Re-check on the day.
- DeepSeek (via Unjailed) is useful for bulk creative/strategy at pennies, but invents claims (loss stats, backstories, prices in posts). Always run a rules review before building. Never send it prospect data.
- DeepSeek given an agent-style brief answers "I'll start by inspecting…" and stops. Tell it plainly it has no tools and must write the deliverables now.
- HyperFrames: audio tags injected by script are dropped at render; write them statically. `render -c variants/x.html` renders non-index entries.

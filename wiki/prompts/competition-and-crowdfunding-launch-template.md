# Competition and Crowdfunding Launch Template

## Core rule

One physically verified product demonstration creates a reusable evidence pack for competitions, client case studies, funding discussions and a later Kickstarter campaign. Each entry gets a small rules-and-theme layer; the product does not accumulate speculative features for each contest.

## Model roles

- Sol handles official-source extraction, CLI evidence, deterministic checks, cleanup, asset production and bounded implementation.
- Astra handles baseline selection, cross-cutting architecture, security/execution boundaries and final skeptical review.
- The founder owns physical filming judgement, eligibility confirmations, pricing and delivery promises, and any platform submission that must be human-authored.

Model changes are planned handoffs. They do not justify pausing safe evidence collection. Each handoff records branch/commit, proofs, failures, protected user data and the next bounded decision.

## Competition record

For every current or future competition, record the official page and terms, deadline/timezone, eligibility, human/agent submission rule, required media/files, judging criteria and weights, theme fit, IP/publicity licence, physical proof gate and submission status.

Reuse:

- one-sentence product story;
- editable connection/architecture diagram;
- component list and build/install instructions;
- physical proof ledger tied to a commit;
- high-resolution device and interaction photographs;
- modular video clips plus a short master demonstration;
- build decisions, failures, fixes and measured limitations;
- sanitized screenshots and client use case;
- licensing, attribution, privacy and secret-scan record;
- BOM and prototype-cost assumptions.

## Kickstarter lane

Kickstarter work follows repeatable physical proof. Before making a campaign promise, define the exact offer and supported hardware, cost BOM/packaging/fulfilment/support/tax/contingency, verify supply and manufacturing assumptions, review privacy/licences/trademarks/claims, build a pre-launch audience, and tie reward quantities and delivery dates to proven capacity.

The MAZ Pocket commercial story is: **capture a thought on a pocket device and turn it into a useful, bounded action on the PC**. Public examples use synthetic client data. Production, compatibility, latency and delivery claims require their own evidence.

## Current MAZ Pocket instantiation

The element14 chain is **Human -> Cardputer ADV -> Wi-Fi -> MAZ Core/PC -> local or cloud AI/agent -> result back to Cardputer**. The competition surface is CALL, CAPTURE, AGENTS and CONTROL. Broader PC automation, radios, sensors, PCB work, telemetry expansion and WORK expansion remain out of scope.

# Someone Should Fix This — Production Brief

## Objective

Build professional proof that can be found by a prospective client. Reach is useful only when it becomes an email subscriber, a practitioner relationship, or an enquiry citing an episode.

## Audience and funnel

- **Reach:** ordinary UK users and public-service practitioners.
- **Clients:** local owners dealing with lost enquiries, repeated admin and fragmented systems.
- **Bridge:** a voluntary mailing-list box beneath each free demo. No demo is gated.

## Fixed franchise contract

Every episode uses this order:

1. Cold open on the observed failure and state the constraint.
2. Put one defensible number on screen.
3. Give the fairness beat between 15 and 25 seconds.
4. Show the build and one rejected approach.
5. Say: “The bit I couldn’t fix…” and give the honest limitation.
6. End: “It’s on the site. The code’s there too.”

Delivery is flat. Keep room tone. No music under speech. No AI voiceover. Use one signature edit per episode.

## Evidence contract

An episode does not ship unless the failure was verified on filming day, every number has a saved primary source, the artefact has a public URL and repository or is labelled prototype, identifiers are hidden, named clients approved exact claims and footage, and the limitation is specific.

Facts, inference and recommendations remain distinct. Refresh tariffs, service status, subreddit rules and product behaviour immediately before publication.

## Release order

Record JobFilter first, field-test and record parking second, prepare Scrap Finance while awaiting permission, and build the synthetic-data quoting prototype as fallback. Publish no more than one main episode in week one. Queue the second. Continue fortnightly with one process post between episodes.

## Measures

Track three-second hold, retention past the fairness beat, completion, episode-page clicks, demo/repo clicks, opt-ins, practitioner comments, surviving Reddit discussion, and enquiries naming the episode. Raw views are diagnostic.

## Human-only gates

Manaz approves domain purchase, profile edits, client contact, location filming, publication and mailing-list activation.

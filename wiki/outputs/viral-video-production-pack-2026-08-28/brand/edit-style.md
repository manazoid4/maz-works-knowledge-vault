# Edit Style Contract

## Sound

Natural voice and room tone. No music under speech. No AI voice. Optional single dry click or stamp only outside speech.

## Picture

Vertical master: 1080 × 1920, 25 or 30 fps, 45–75 seconds. Evidence in frame one. Captions use sentence case, two lines maximum, and one highlighted operative word. Blur plates, faces, emails, names, account IDs and notifications.

## One signature effect each

- Parking: signal bars fall to zero while the pay sign stays pinned.
- JobFilter: notices visibly sort into accepted/rejected lanes.
- Scrap Finance: enquiry travels through ownership/status stages.
- Quoting: duplicated fields collapse into one record.

## End card

Black background, white type, small yellow episode number. Spoken and shown: “It’s on the site. The code’s there too.” Hold 1.5 seconds.

## Inspiration translated

Fixed franchise beats; visible constraint; named Derby locations; committed civic deadpan; result before mechanism; three hook takes; immediate ending after proof and limitation. Borrow mechanics, not personas or production workload.

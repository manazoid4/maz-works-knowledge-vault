# Adversarial Post-Mortem

Assume eight weeks passed and no clients arrived.

## Collapse points

1. **The parking fix is theatre.** It stores codes but cannot complete offline payment. Call it an offline reference, not a payment replacement.
2. **Proof overreaches.** Scrap Finance workspace is called live. Separate shipped site from coded prototype.
3. **Deadpan becomes low energy.** If viewers leave before fairness, show proof earlier and shorten pauses.
4. **Reach misses buyers.** Follow parking with product/client proof; track enquiries, not views.
5. **Editing consumes delivery.** One signature effect per episode.
6. **Reddit sees promotion.** Do not launch in research communities.
7. **Compliance fails.** Keep list off until consent, privacy and unsubscribe work; blur identifiers; get permission.
8. **Artefacts fail scrutiny.** Test public URL and fresh-clone setup before export.

## Weakest episode

The real-business episode is strategically strongest and operationally weakest. It needs trust, permission, safe footage and a working fix. Do not force it into week one. The synthetic quoting episode is fallback, not fake client proof.

## If episode one flops

Inspect first-frame hold and fairness-beat retention. Re-cut only the opening if proof arrives late. Publish the second controlled episode. Do not rewrite the premise after one upload.

## Eight-week value test

Continue only if the series produces a relevant enquiry, repeat practitioner engagement, owned-audience conversion after the compliant list exists, or case-study assets that improve direct outreach. Otherwise keep the assets and move the hours to permission-based outreach.

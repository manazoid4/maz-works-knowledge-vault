# Research Decisions

## Four formats

1. **Pain → Tiny Win:** open mid-problem, hard cut to a visible result, no logo sting.
2. **Outcome-First Demo:** finished output in frame one, then only the two or three interactions that caused it.
3. **Founder/Build Story:** founder voice/face, one specific frustration, screen proof, and a clear “who this is for” ending.
4. **Six-Scene Curiosity Story:** reuse the skeleton format's six-beat escalation and continuity system, but replace skeleton imagery with a product-native motif.

## Editing language

- Click-magnet zoom only on decisive interactions: roughly 1.0 → 1.35 scale over 8–12 frames.
- Spotlight mask: dim irrelevant UI 35–50% while preserving the active control.
- Kinetic caption packets: two to five words; one semantic word in the brand accent.
- Proof-card spring: freeze/dim the result and animate one metric/result card 0.84 → 1.04 → 1.0.
- One state-change flash or wipe per video, reserved for the actual transformation.
- One restrained sound motif across the pack: click ticks, short riser, one proof impact.

## Production stack

- Primary: Cap Studio for screen recording, click-centred zooms, vertical canvas, cursor cleanup, highlights, captions, and scene switching.
- Caption correction: Subtitle Edit.
- Talking-head dead-air option: Auto-Editor, with manual review and 0.2-second margins.
- Repeatable overlays: a minimal Remotion package only if the artifact justifies coded timers/proof cards.
- Optional utilities: LosslessCut and Kdenlive.
- Do not make ClipForge the backbone: its alpha package is not on PyPI, has Windows blockers, documentation/API mismatches, no tests, randomized renders, and unverified cost/virality claims.

## Automation boundary

Automate naming, manifests, reusable overlays, rough captions, and asset generation. Keep human approval for claims, emotional delivery, crop, captions, timing, and publishing. Any automation must enforce maximum six scenes, one retry per asset, duration limits, missing-asset failure, and manual publish.

## Measurement

Use engaged views, stayed-to-watch/viewed-versus-swiped, average percentage viewed, retention dips/spikes, saves/comments, and CTA actions. Raw Shorts views are not the primary comparison because starts and replays now count as views.

## Claim taxonomy

- Product fact: must be supported and visually demonstrated.
- Creator anecdote: label as anecdotal.
- Experiment hypothesis: test it; never state it as a guarantee.

# Script

**0–7s — failure + constraint**  
“A finance firm asked for a website. The website could collect an enquiry. It could not make a person own the next action. I used dummy records only.”

**7–15s — number**  
“One form submission creates one question: who is doing what next?”

**15–24s — fairness**  
“An inbox is not automatically broken. For a small team it may be enough. It fails when ownership and status exist only in somebody’s head.”

**24–48s — build**  
“The site is shipped. Then I coded a workspace for source, qualification context, ownership, templates, history, approval and suppression. The remote setup is not live, and sending stays disabled. I rejected calling code a deployed operation.”

**48–57s — limitation**  
“The bit I couldn’t fix is the result after handover. I do not have verified lead or revenue outcomes, so I will not invent them.”

**57–61s — sign-off**  
“It’s on the site. The code’s there too.”

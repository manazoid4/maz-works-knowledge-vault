# Script

**0–7s — failure + constraint**  
“The job ends at five. The quoting starts at eight. I used one fake customer, one fake job and no automatic sending.”

**7–15s — number**  
“The same job appears in three places: message, notes and quote.”

**15–24s — fairness**  
“A spreadsheet is not a failure. For many trades it is the right tool. The waste starts when the same facts are retyped and nobody can see the next action.”

**24–47s — build**  
“I made one intake record produce a draft quote and a visible status. The owner reviews it and presses send. I rejected fully automatic quoting because site conditions and judgement do not fit safely into a form.”

**47–56s — limitation**  
“The bit I couldn’t fix is whether the inputs are true. A cleaner workflow cannot inspect the job.”

**56–60s — sign-off**  
“It’s on the site. The code’s there too.”

# Audit of the Claude Campaign Plan

## Verdict

The strategy is strong, but it mixes durable principles, unverified claims, future work and publishing instructions. This pack keeps the franchise and proof logic, removes unsupported certainty, and gates production.

## Keep

- Reach lane, client lane and owned-list bridge.
- Constraint-led opening, fairness beat, limitation and fixed sign-off.
- Hyperlocal filming, inspectable artefacts and honest limitations.
- Reddit for discovery and language research, not link dropping.
- Proof hierarchy and fortnightly cadence.

## Correct

- Parking: 73% of surveyed parking-app users reported difficulty; among those, 70% cited unreliable signal. Do not say 70% could not pay.
- A planned DWP successor does not prove a current interface failure.
- LinkedIn supports Page completeness; it does not prove a universal personal-v-company reach ratio. Personal-first is a campaign choice.
- Scrap Finance’s site is shipped. Workspace/outbound controls exist in code, but its repository says the remote Supabase migration is not applied and live email is disabled. Do not call those parts live.
- A domain helps but should not block filming controlled proof.

## Cut or defer

- DWP episode until a direct, countable failure is verified.
- Complaint subreddits as launch channels; several current rules make them research-only.
- “90/10” described as official Reddit policy.
- Newsletter engineering inside this filming sprint; it is a separate consent/privacy/delivery task.
- Announcing the series before an episode exists.

## Add

- Claim ledger and filming-day refresh.
- Permission gates and dependency-free fallback.
- Exact scripts, shot lists, edit maps and validator.

## Main change

Record JobFilter first. Parking has higher reach potential but depends on location, signal, current signs and a truthful offline artefact.

# Viral Video Production Pack

This is the execution pack for the first four episodes of **Someone Should Fix This**. It replaces the speculative campaign brief with scripts, evidence gates, shot lists, edit maps, publishing copy, a seven-day sprint, and validation scripts.

## What to make in the next seven days

1. **JobFilter: the honest zero result** — film first because the product, code and failure state are controlled and inspectable.
2. **Scrap Finance: the website was not the whole problem** — film second only with written client permission and with the workspace described as coded, not live.
3. **Parking: app-only, no signal** — field-test after controlled product proof; film only after measuring the failure and verifying the sign, tariff and code.
4. **The job ends at five; quoting starts at eight** — a synthetic-data prototype for a real trade workflow; replace with a consenting local business when available.

The proposed DWP episode is deferred. Official sources confirm a successor to Find a Job is planned, but current research does not support a specific, countable user-facing failure strongly enough.

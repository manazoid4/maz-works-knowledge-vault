# Execution Plan — Research Complete

The research and production-folder tasks are complete. Physical capture now follows [[../production/seven-day-sprint]].

## Outcome

Produce four beginner-manageable, 9:16 videos whose scripts, footage, edits, claims, captions, and measurement plans can be executed without interpreting a strategy document during production.

## Dependency order

```text
artifact export
  → original-plan audit + inspiration inventory
    → product/audience/claim evidence sheet
      → shared edit language
        → four video packets
          → batch recording
            → editing + QA
              → publishing experiment + post-mortem
```

## Task 1: Ingest the original artifact

**Acceptance criteria**

- The original brief is stored locally as source material.
- Every inspiration example has a title, URL/creator where available, and a description of the reusable technique.
- Product, audience, desired action, constraints, and factual claims are extractable without guessing.

**Verification:** every final brief decision can cite either the source artifact or the research ledger.

## Task 2: Audit before rewriting

Score the plan on audience specificity, first-frame promise, proof, feasibility, edit specificity, production burden, factual support, distribution, measurement, and reuse.

**Acceptance criteria**

- Findings are separated into keep, cut, clarify, and add.
- Each criticism has a concrete replacement.
- No “viral” claim is presented as guaranteed.

## Task 3: Lock the shared creative system

Choose one caption system, one brand accent, one proof-card animation, one sound palette, and one project-native visual motif.

**Acceptance criteria**

- No video uses more than three signature motifs.
- The product/result remains the highest-contrast object.
- Every effect has a narrative purpose and a low-effort implementation path.

## Task 4: Write four vertical slices

1. Pain → Tiny Win, 12–20 seconds.
2. Outcome-First Demo, 15–25 seconds.
3. Founder/Build Story, 20–30 seconds.
4. Six-Scene Curiosity Story, 25–40 seconds.

Each packet must include exact hook and ending copy, body beats, timestamped capture actions, crop/zoom targets, caption packets, SFX cues, required assets, export name, and definition of done.

## Task 5: Production checkpoint

- Film three hook takes per concept.
- Batch A-roll, screen recordings, hands/reactions, and pickups separately.
- Use `scene-01` through `scene-06` naming where applicable.
- Fail fast on missing proof footage or unsupported claims.

## Task 6: Edit and QA

- Understandable muted from frame one.
- One promise and one demonstrated payoff per video.
- Meaningful clicks only receive zoom/highlight treatment.
- Captions fit platform safe areas.
- 1080×1920 H.264 master exported with no unlicensed media.

## Task 7: Publish as an experiment

Create two hook variants per concept while holding the body constant. Compare engaged views/stayed-to-watch, average percentage viewed, retention dips/spikes, saves/comments, and actual CTA actions. Do not optimize against raw Shorts starts alone.

## Task 8: Post-mortem

After 48 hours and again after seven days, record what was expected, what happened, where viewers left or replayed, which comments revealed confusion/demand, what took too long, and the single change to carry into the next batch.
